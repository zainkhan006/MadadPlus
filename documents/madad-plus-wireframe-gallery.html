9 · Design System (Foundation)

Tokens used across all wireframes above — ready to hand to Figma.

Colors

Emergency Red#E63946 · urgent actions only
Trust Blue#1D3557 · primary, branding
Service Green#2A9D8F · domestic, success
Warning Orange#F4A261 · approaching, caution
Neutral Gray#6C757D · secondary, offline
Light Gray#F8F9FA · backgrounds

Status Language

🟢 Available 🔴 Busy ⚫ Offline 🔵 En Route 🟠 Approaching

Typography: Inter (Roboto fallback · Noto Nastaliq Urdu for Urdu) — H1 24 · H2 20 · Body 16 · Critical numbers (ETA) 32, weight 700
Buttons: Primary 48px / Emergency 56px (taller = urgency) · radius 8px   Cards: white, radius 12px, padding 16px
Accessibility: contrast ≥ 4.5:1 · touch targets ≥ 48px · never color alone (icon + text) · reduced-motion support
Madad+ — FYP Demo. All ambulance organizations, vehicles, providers, and requests shown are synthetic. No real emergency response is provided or implied. In a real emergency, call 1122.
